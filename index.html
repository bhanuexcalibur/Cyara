04 / Competitive Playbook

Straight Answers for Sellers: Where Cyara Wins and Where We Concede

Equipping enterprise account executives with definitive objection reframing.

✓ WHERE WE WIN (THE CORE MOAT)
PSTN voice reality and telephony handoff. Pure-play eval tools test text prompts in Python environments. They cannot dial telephone numbers, test speech-to-text degradation under background noise, validate carrier audio quality, or confirm live transfer to human CCaaS queues.
− WHERE WE CONCEDE (DO NOT FIGHT)
Individual developers testing internal chat prototypes in GitHub. For raw developer prompt-engineering experiments, open-source CLI tools are faster and cheaper. We concede individual dev playground seats to focus on production enterprise risk.
The Seller Reframe
"Promptfoo tests your prompt syntax in GitHub. Cyara tests whether your live customer hears the right answer over a degraded mobile cell tower."
✓ WHERE WE WIN (THE CORE MOAT)
Multi-vendor neutrality and cross-platform assurance. Enterprises rarely run single-vendor ecosystems. They use AWS Connect for voice, Salesforce Agentforce for CRM, and custom LLM microservices. Native CCaaS testing cannot assure the journey across external tools.
− WHERE WE CONCEDE (DO NOT FIGHT)
Single-vendor captive shop bundled pricing. If a customer is fully committed to a single CCaaS vendor and only runs simple bundled bots with no external LLMs, native testing is often bundled at nominal cost.
The Seller Reframe
"CCaaS native tools grade their own homework. Cyara is your independent auditor across voice, digital, and custom LLM agents."
✓ WHERE WE WIN (THE CORE MOAT)
Pre-production coverage and continuous automated vigilance. Manual QA evaluates only a small fraction of interactions days after the customer interaction occurred. Cyara validates synthetic journeys before launch and continuously monitors live production traffic in real time.
− WHERE WE CONCEDE (DO NOT FIGHT)
Highly subjective executive relationship management. Nuanced human negotiations and VIP white-glove dispute resolution still benefit from experienced human empathy reviews.
The Seller Reframe
"Post-call manual sampling tells you which customer got hurt yesterday. Cyara stops the bad agent from answering the phone today."
